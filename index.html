    About us

    The Department of Computer Science & Engineering (AIML) at the Institute of Engineering & Management, Kolkata, teaches machine learning, data science and software development through a four-year B.Tech programme.

    EventPulse is a student-built website for the department's events. It was created as an IT Workshop (PCCCS382) project using only HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript.
